About

About

SocialSave

Version 1.0.0

SocialSave is a download manager for public video URLs that you are allowed to save. This web app never bypasses DRM, logins, or private posts. Demo mode uses a sample Creative Commons MP4 so the flow works without scraping social platforms.

gavel
You are responsible for the content you download. SocialSave only works with publicly accessible media that you are allowed to save.

Privacy

SocialSave is a local download manager. Settings and download history stay in this browser unless you share a file yourself.

What stays on this device

Theme, quality, Wi-Fi-only preference, notifications, and history (title, source URL, platform, date, size). Downloaded files are saved through your browser.

What is not collected

This demo does not send URLs to a backend, create accounts, or store watch history on a server.

Legal / compliance

By using SocialSave you agree to save only videos that are publicly accessible and that you have the right to download.

Permitted use

Public links, Creative Commons media, and files the source platform allows you to keep.

Prohibited use

Do not use SocialSave to bypass DRM, logins, private accounts, paywalls, or other access controls, or to copy content you do not have permission to save.

Responsibility

You are responsible for the URLs you paste and the files you save. SocialSave does not grant a copyright license in third-party content.
