# Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-04-09

SocialSave is a local download manager. It is designed to keep as little personal data as possible.

## What the app stores on your device

- Settings such as theme, default quality, and Wi-Fi-only downloads
- Download history: title, source URL, platform, local file path, date, size, and duration
- Downloaded video files you choose to save
- Cached thumbnails

This data stays on your device unless you share a file yourself.

## What the app sends to the backend

When you tap Analyze or Download, the app sends the URL you pasted to your configured backend over HTTPS. The backend may also receive a public client token if you configured one.

The app does not send your contacts, photos, location, or account passwords.

## What the backend should not store

The example backend is designed not to keep user accounts, watch history, or copies of downloaded videos longer than needed to fulfill a request. Temporary download tokens expire.

## Permissions

- Network access is required to analyze URLs and download files.
- Storage or photo-library permission is requested only if you choose the public Downloads location.
- Notifications are optional.

## Third parties

SocialSave does not scrape private social-media accounts. Platform metadata is requested only through official or otherwise permitted APIs configured on the server. Those platforms have their own privacy policies.
